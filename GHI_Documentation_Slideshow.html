System Overview

Global Health Intelligence (GHI) System

Public Health Authority

The Global Health Intelligence (GHI) System is an advanced, automated event-based epidemiological surveillance platform engineered for the Public Health Authority (وقاية).

It aggregates, parses, triages, and evaluates global health signals across 45 primary surveillance networks to detect infectious disease outbreaks, CBRN incidents, and environmental health threats in real time.

07 // 08

International Health Regulations (IHR) Matrix

  • • Question 1: Is the public health impact serious?
  • • Question 2: Is the event unusual or unexpected?
  • • Question 3: Is there a significant risk of international spread?
  • • Question 4: Is there a significant risk of travel/trade restrictions?

WHO Rapid Risk Assessment (RRA) Engine

  • • Hazard Assessment: Pathogenicity, transmissibility & CFR
  • • Exposure Assessment: Population contact & vector dynamics
  • • Context Assessment: Healthcare system capacity & resources
  • • Overall Risk Rating: Low / Moderate / High / Very High
Ensures national response decisions align strictly with WHO and IHR guidelines Public Health Authority
