Signal Detection

Media scanning is an active process performed by the assigned EIOS’s team daily, during workweek (between 09:00 to 10:00 AM) to detect the signals.

Signal filtration

Signals that are captured shall be filtered by removing duplicates and information that is not relevant 
Signals shall be reported to EI unit for Triaging.
SS Specialist

4
Signal Triaging
Triaging round will be conducted by PHI EIOS team daily.
Picked up signals shall meet one of the following criteria
Unknown illness 
Unusual event (antimicrobial resistance, detection of new features of a disease, atypical signs, and symptoms, significantly high number of cases over a short period in a limited geographic area, newly imported disease, occurrence in an area outside the endemic areas in the country)
The risk to the health system (affects a significant proportion of health workers, Case fatality rate (CFR) of the disease is significantly higher than expected, the occurrence of disease outside the normal seasonal pattern, re-emergence of communicable diseases, affects a conflict area)
International disease spread
Interference with travel or trade
Disease for elimination or eradication 
Zoonotic diseases
Suspected, accidental or deliberate biological or chemical threats
Sensational report or high media attention expected
SS Specialist
5
Second Signal Triaging


A second triaging process to identify signals of interests.
Signals triaged at this stage are to be included in the weekly report for stakeholders to review.
Signals inclusion criteria are:
Emerging; new pathogen or strain
Diseases with High CFR; e.g., Haemorrhagic fevers
Diseases with high publicity (Frequently mentioned)
Events in neighbouring countries
Chemical hazards and products that potentially be imported
Important Respiratory diseases Monitoring


SS Health Expert

follow-up on signals sent for verification until verification is obtained. Signals should be verified within 24 hours of detection.

Report any signal that requires escalation to Director, Syndromic Surveillance

Appendix 1 — EIOS Daily Article Screening Strategy
Section
Procedure (Daily Screening)
A. Purpose
Standardize daily EIOS screening for Infectious Disease Surveillance and Environmental Threats Surveillance using consistent boards, filters, categories, and agreed keywords (aligned with epi-week).
1. Access & boards
Open EIOS at: https://eios.who.int/portal/monitoring/boards 
Select a board; Use two boards: 1) Infectious Disease Surveillance; 2) Environmental Threats Surveillance.

2. Basic filters
Apply baseline filters within the selected board: 
Time (previous day or 3 days).
Language (English and/or Arabic) if needed.
Geographical area (When monitoring targeted event is required)

2.1 Time (epi-week aligned)
Epi-week runs Sunday 00:00 to Saturday 23:59. 
Daily screening uses previous day only: 00:01–23:59 of the previous calendar day. 
Sunday screening covers Thursday 00:01 through Saturday 23:59. 
When needed and approved, a process for extra hours may be required. This ensures continuous epi-week coverage without overlap or gaps. 
2.2 Language
Select publication language(s): English and/or Arabic (as applicable).
3. Infectious Disease board
3.1 Category: Categories → Human Health → Human Infectious Disease. 

3.2 Browse stream without Text Search keywords. 
3.3 Text Search (one keyword per search): Outbreak; Cluster; Epidemic; outbreak; Unusual; Sudden increase; Spike in cases; Mysterious; Mystery/Unknown disease; Mass/Unusual deaths; Hospital overload; Overwhelmed hospitals; Quarantine/Lockdown; surge; Critical; Hospitalized/Admission surge; Death toll/Fatalities rising. 
3.4 Disease-specific searches (as needed): Meningococcal meningitis (Neisseria meningitidis); Meningitis (other); COVID-19; Influenza; MERS; Mpox; Dengue; Viral hemorrhagic fevers (e.g., Ebola); Yellow fever; Marburg; AFP/suspected poliomyelitis; Cholera; Foodborne outbreak; Malaria; Measles.
4. Environmental Threats board
4.1 Select Environmental Threats Surveillance board and apply Time + Language filters. 
4.2 Category: Categories → Human Health → Social and environmental factors → environmental or toxic exposures

4.3 Category: Categories → CBRN and HAZMAT → CBRN, chemical hazard/event, radio nuclear hazards/event and unknown substances
4.4 Category: Categories → chemicals → all chemicals; unknown substance

4.5 Category: Categories → environment → natural disasters and pollution

4.6 Browse without Text Search keywords.
4.7 Text Search (one keyword per search): Poisoning; Emergency; risk; disaster; Chemical; Radiation; Nuclear.
